Npltz is a clean, native Nepali (Bikram Sambat) calendar for Android, built with Java and Material 3.

- Month and year views with the matching AD date under each day
- Day details, week number, days remaining and year progress
- Events and notes with optional reminder times
- Daily date notification and an optional permanent notification
- Calendar marks for the Nepali New Year, the Gregorian New Year
- Date, month and greeting home-screen widgets
- Nine Material You themes, three fonts, four text sizes and English/Nepali scripts

Everything runs offline. The BS calendar data is compiled into the app, so there is no API, no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The table covers 1975–2090 BS.

Npltz started as a TUI app for desktop and Termux. This Android app shares the same calendar logic and design language.

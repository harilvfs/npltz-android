Bikram Sambat calendar for Android

The Nepali calendar, without the noise.

Npltz shows today's BS date, the matching AD date, events and reminders in a small, native Android app. Nothing to sign into, nothing to load, nothing tracking you.

Features

A calendar, and not much else.

Everything you need to keep track of Bikram Sambat dates, and nothing you don't.

Month and year views

Scan a full BS month or the whole year. Every day carries its matching AD date underneath.

Day details

Open any date for the week number, days remaining and year progress at a glance.

Events and notes

Attach events and notes to a date, with an optional reminder time.

Notifications

A daily date notification, plus an optional permanent one that keeps today's date in your shade.

Home screen widgets

Date, month and greeting widgets, all drawn with your current theme.

Themes and type

Nine color themes, three fonts, four text sizes, English and Nepali scripts.

Offline by design

The BS calendar data is compiled into the app, covering 1975 to 2090 BS. There is no API, no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission in the manifest. Nothing leaves your phone.
